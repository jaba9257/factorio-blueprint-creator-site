Original Factorio graphics

Factorio 2.1.23 icons and models are included with the editor and load automatically. Required images are cached in your browser separately from your blueprints. You do not need to install the game or upload graphics files.

If images do not appear

  1. Wait for loading to finish. The top bar will show “Graphics: automatic”.
  2. Open “Graphics” and choose “Retry loading graphics” or “Check for graphics updates”.
  3. Check your network connection. Placeholders remain available when resources cannot be loaded; you can still create and export blueprints.

Images also load automatically on other devices or addresses. Clearing browser data removes the cache, which will be populated again. Previously viewed frames may remain available during a connection failure; full offline use is not guaranteed. Download a separate project file regularly to back up your work.

What is displayed

159 entity definitions for compatibility, 119 entities in the current Space Age catalog, original quality and recipe icons, static directional sprites, belt corners, pipe fittings, connected cargo floors, scale, offsets, and shadows. Animation, working lights, smoke, and simulation are not included. Image dimensions do not determine occupied tiles.
